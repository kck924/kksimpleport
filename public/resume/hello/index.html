Hi there, …and thanks for being curious enough about me as a candidate to find this page. I’ve created this page to express a little bit about why I’m currently approaching the process of resume distribution in this AI-forward way. I promise that every word and every idea on this page came direct from my fingertips — no AI middleware here. Yep, even that emdash in the last sentence was me.

So why am I doing things this way? Well for starters, because I’ve spent a lot of time on the other side of the hiring process, and I’m fortunate enough to have obtained a feel for the market that way. I’m a hiring manager at a large global tech company, and have posted and filled several positions in the last six months. I’ve seen the way a job can attract thousands of applicants in days, and I’ve seen the way that lifeless AI-resumes dominate the pile but do manage a reasonable degree of success navigating the automatic qualification check systems. Marketplaces are two-sided, and the applicant side of the job market has clearly dictated that it’s a numbers game.

But still, that’s a lousy experience on the hiring side. It feels at best impersonal, at worst actively dishonest. And yet, this is the way the wind is blowing. So how can I put the wind at my back in a way that doesn’t compromise my values? I’m a stats guy, so I view the notion of there being a “numbers game” component to this as something of an immutable truth. Within that constraint, however, I see the opportunity to stand out by simultaneously showcasing effective application of this nascent, powerful technology, while still preserving humanity of thought and experience for all involved.

That’s why my website has pictures of my dog on it, and my digital projects are all accompanied by my actual physical handwriting. Because as interested as I am in growing technically in the age of AI, and expanding my understanding of what teams, individuals, and organizations are capable of achieving, human heart needs to still be pumping the lifeblood.

Thanks again for finding this page, and if I sound like someone you’re interested in having a conversation with, you know how to get ahold of me!
